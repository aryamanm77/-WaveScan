Crucial Step Right After Web-Flashing:

When the flasher says "Installation complete", press the EN (or RST) button on your ESP32 once (or unplug and replug the USB cable). This reboots the ESP32 out of flash mode and immediately broadcasts the WΛVΞSCAN Wi-Fi network!
